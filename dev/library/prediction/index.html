Prediction

After running inference, use predict_GP to compute the posterior predictive distribution at new test inputs.

Predictive distribution

RxGPs.predict_GP — Function
predict_GP(; m_in, q_v, q_θ, meta::UniSGPMeta) -> (means, covs)

The posterior predictive distribution of the GP, under the operator of meta, at the test inputs m_in, given the marginal q_v of the inducing variables (Eq. 30 of the dID-VSGP paper):

\[\mu_* = \tilde{L}m(x_*) + \tilde{K}_{*u}\,(\mu_v - K_{uu}^{-1} m_u), \qquad \Sigma_* = \tilde{K}_{**} + \tilde{K}_{*u}\,(\Sigma_v - K_{uu}^{-1})\,\tilde{K}_{*u}^\top.\]

Keywords

  • m_in: the test inputs, a vector of vectors of length D (for D = 1, [[x] for x in xs]).
  • q_v: the marginal of the inducing variables, a multivariate normal.
  • q_θ: the kernel hyperparameters: a vector, or a distribution whose mean is used.
  • meta: the UniSGPMeta the model ran with, built at the same θ.

Returns

means, a vector with the predictive mean at each test input, and covs, its covariance. Each mean has length P, the output dimension of the operator: 1 for :fn (so the function's mean at input i is means[i][1]), D for :grad and 1 + D for :joint_fn_grad; each covariance is P×P.

source

The posterior predictive at a test point $x_*$ is:

\[\mu_{f_*} = \tilde{L}\,m(x_*) + \tilde{K}_{x_*u}\bigl(\mu_v - K_{uu}^{-1}\mathbf{m}_u\bigr)\]

\[\Sigma_{f_*} = \tilde{K}_{x_*x_*} + \tilde{K}_{x_*u}\bigl(\Sigma_v - K_{uu}^{-1}\bigr)\tilde{K}_{x_*u}^\top\]

where:

  • $\tilde{L}$ is the linear operator encoded in the meta (identity, gradient, or joint)
  • $\mu_v, \Sigma_v$ are the posterior mean and covariance of the inducing variable $\mathbf{v}$
  • $\mathbf{m}_u$ collects the prior mean at the inducing locations

The output dimensionality $P$ at each test point depends on the operator:

Operator$P$means[i]covs[i]
:fn11-vector1×1 matrix
:grad$D$$D$-vector$D \times D$ matrix
:joint_fn_grad$1+D$$(1+D)$-vector$(1+D) \times (1+D)$ matrix

Example

Getting started runs predict_GP on a fitted regression and plots its mean and $2\sigma$ band, and the usage examples do the same after learning the hyperparameters. The meta passed to predict_GP must be built at the same θ as q_θ.
